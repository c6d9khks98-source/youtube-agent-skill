## Recurring cast (reuse in every episode, same words every prompt)

- **Dilly Duck** (hero): a small round bright-yellow duckling, orange beak and feet,
  big shiny black eyes, a little sky-blue bow on top of her head. Bouncy and brave.
- **Pip, Pop, Puff and Peep**: her four siblings, pale-yellow ducklings with orange
  beaks, no bow. Pip has a green scarf, Pop a red cap, Puff is extra fluffy, Peep is smallest.
- **Bo the Frog**: a round lime-green frog with big friendly eyes, sits on a mossy log.
- **Mama Duck**: a big white duck with an orange beak and a soft pink neckerchief.
- **Set: Puddle Pond**: a sunny cartoon pond with a mossy log, lily pads, cattails,
  round green bushes and a little wooden dock. Bright glossy 3D toddler style.
