# kids-bible.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/youtube/kids-bible.md` and fill it in, or run `/yt-kids bible` and answer
the questions. `/yt-kids` reads it before every episode. Character descriptions are copied word for
word into every generation, so a vague line here becomes a different-looking character in every
episode.

## Never show

- Peril that is not resolved in the same episode
- A character hurt for a laugh
- Anything unsafe a child could copy
- Brand names, real people
- Scary or distorted faces in a thumbnail
